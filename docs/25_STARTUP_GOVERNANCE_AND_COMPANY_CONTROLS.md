# 25 — Startup Governance and Company Controls

This product is intended to become a real Pakistani mobility business, so company readiness is part of system readiness. Software completion does not satisfy these controls.

## Ownership and corporate records
Company formation/name reservation, founder/share ownership, cap table, IP assignment, domains/trademarks, bank/merchant accounts and authorized signatories must have real records and qualified advice. SECP/FBR/PRA requirements are time- and fact-specific; docs list blockers rather than pretending to give legal clearance.

## People and access
Before any staff/contractors handle production or rider/driver information: agreement, role, confidentiality/privacy/security training, least privilege, MFA and auditable onboarding. Departures or role changes revoke GitHub/cloud/store/support/PSP/social/physical access promptly.

## Finance and runway
Maintain a chart of accounts and evidence-backed close; reconcile company bank/PSP/cash/driver payable/platform commission/refunds/promos/vendor spend. Maintain a rolling runway model and separate growth marketing from safety/support/compliance capacity. Never use customer/driver custodial funds as company runway.

## Vendor/procurement
Every material vendor has owner, commercial terms, data/credential map, renewal/expiry, SLA/support, incident contact and exit path. Free-tier accounts are not anonymously owned by developers and must be transferable to company custody before launch.

## Physical operations
Driver/support centers need document privacy, staff/visitor controls, anti-bribery/override controls, secure cash policy if cash is ever collected, safety escalation and secure disposal. Spreadsheets/local photos must not become shadow KYC databases.

## Fundraising
Fundraising starts from verified traction/economics, not inflated claims. Investor data rooms expose company evidence while minimizing rider/driver personal information. Cap table/IP/security/legal gaps are diligence blockers, not presentation problems.

## Governance principle
Founder remains final product/business authority during bootstrap, but high-impact money, identity, safety and production-access changes use maker-checker/segregation of duties as staffing permits and become stricter as the team grows.
