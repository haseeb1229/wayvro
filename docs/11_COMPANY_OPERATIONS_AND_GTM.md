# 11 — Company Formation, Operations and Go-To-Market

## Company setup (obtain jurisdiction-specific counsel)
- Brand/domain/trademark availability review and name reservation.
- Single-member/private limited structure decision (SECP eZfile), share ownership/founder rights, accounting practices, NTN/tax/beneficial owner/returns as applicable.
- Bank account/merchant onboarding, banking/EMI/PSP agreements; liability and ride-hailing insurance; transport/vehicle permits & inspections.
- Privacy policy, rider/driver terms, driver partner contracts, fleet contracts, fare/change/cancellation rules, copyright/open-source notices, dispute procedures.
- External security assessment, incident response retainers, accessible customer support.

## Operations organization (people + process)
- Field driver acquisition, verification training and document renewal;
- Daily online driver supply and dispatch monitoring;
- Customer/driver support ticketing; safety emergency escalation and police/emergency liaison;
- Driver payout and cash commission settlement, failed payout review;
- Vehicle checks, insurance renewal, fleet partner monitoring;
- Fraud investigation with evidence integrity and staff confidentiality;
- Product analytics, QA feedback and release/change management;
- Finance/tax closing, legal/compliance updates and privacy requests.

## Founder metrics dashboards
- Marketplace: rider first-search success, pickup wait, ride completion, no-driver ratio, demand/supply by 1km hex/corridor, effective available drivers.
- Driver: verified active supply, net earning/hour, acceptance/cancellation, payout delay, churn.
- Rider: activation, second-ride retention, median ETA, conversion, repeat booking, complaint rate, cohort economics.
- Finance: gross bookings, platform contribution per trip, subsidy/CAC, driver payable, unpaid commissions, refunds, fraud loss, support cost.
- Safety: incident/100k trips, severe incident response, audit outcomes, KYC expiration, appeal resolution.

## Lahore controlled-launch concept (recommendation, not a commitment)
1. Pick one manageable service area/corridor where field support can physically operate.
2. Interview and onboard small verified driver cohort, document all legal and insurance requirements.
3. Run shadow dispatch with synthetic trip events, then invited rider closed beta with real support.
4. Demonstrate availability and reliable payments/receipts/refunds before public ads.
5. Expand to adjacent zones only if density, safety, retention and contribution margin are trending adequately.
6. Later evaluate women driver corridors/commute pooling only after vetted supply and dedicated policy.

## Marketing
Original brand, honest trust messaging, verified safety practices, driver economics transparency, university/business partnerships, referrals with anti-fraud, local launch ambassadors, paid channels only after service meets expectations. Avoid massive acquisition spend before sufficient drivers; ads without supply destroy trust.

## Funding roadmap
Bootstrap product prototype -> traction and unit-economics report -> angel/pre-seed conversations as justified -> transparent cap table, financial projections, data room, investor diligence. Funding is not guaranteed; build a viable low-subsidy service first.

## Field validation gates
Write SOPs, insure and authorize transport, support available, conduct crisis drill, publish accurate marketing, check store compliance and provider contracts, test payment reconciliation and rollback. A mock demo is not a public launch.
