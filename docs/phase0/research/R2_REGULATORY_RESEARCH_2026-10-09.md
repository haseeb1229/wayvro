## A. Punjab TNC / ride-hailing regulation (most important)

1. **Platform licence exists and is being issued.** Yango Ride received the first "Transport Network Company (TNC) operating licence" from the Punjab Provincial Transport Authority (PTA-Punjab), announced 17–18 Mar 2026, "under the Provincial Motor Vehicle (Amendment) Act, 2025" (company statement). *[Closure note: incumbent-specific licence status is SECONDARY_REPORT / NEEDS_OFFICIAL_RECONCILIATION against the official Punjab Transport Department register; it is not treated as law (CL-21).]* Sources: Daily Pakistan 18-Mar-2026 (https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/18-Mar-2026/yango-becomes-first-ride-hailing-firm-to-secure-tnc-licence-in-punjab); Business Recorder (https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40412065, 403 to fetch; seen in search snippet); The News (https://www.thenews.pk/print/1405250-yango-secures-first-tnc-licence-in-punjab).
2. **Statutory basis.** Search snippets of punjablaws.gov.pk/laws/189.html (Provincial Motor Vehicles Ordinance 1965, Punjab consolidated) show a definition of "TNC", Chapter V-B, **s.72-H** (no TNC may operate without a PTA licence; application with fee per Sixteenth Schedule, incorporation certificate, MoA/AoA, passenger safety/security protocol commitment, complaints mechanism), **s.72-M** and a **Sixteenth Schedule** (grant/renewal fee). A search snippet quoted **grant fee Rs 2,000,000 valid 3 years; renewal Rs 1,000,000 valid 1 year** — **UNVERIFIED** (page 404 on direct fetch). The Transport Department's consolidated Ordinance PDF dated 30-11-2023 (https://transport.punjab.gov.pk/urdu/system/files/The%20Provincial%20Motor%20Vehicles%20Ordinance,%201965%20(30-11-2023).pdf) contains **no** "transport network", "44-B" or Sixteenth Schedule text, so the TNC chapter and s.44B were inserted after Nov 2023, consistent with a 2025 amendment. The amending Act's gazette text was not found on pap.gov.pk.
3. **TNC Safety & Security Protocols notification (primary, 19 Feb 2026).** Listed on transport.punjab.gov.pk/notifications (https://transport.punjab.gov.pk/system/files/TNC%20Safety%20and%20Security%20Protocols.pdf). Downloaded (2.8 MB) but it is a **scanned image with no text layer**; not OCR'd. **Contents UNVERIFIED — the single most important document for counsel to read.** Likely to carry driver-verification / panic-button / data-sharing conditions attached to the licence.
4. **Section 44B driver/vehicle route permits (reported late Jul 2026).** Dawn 28-Jul-2026 (https://www.dawn.com/news/2018867), ProPakistani 28-Jul-2026, PakWheels 29-Jul-2026, Business Recorder, TechJuice, Pakistan Observer report Punjab brought ride-hailing under the MVO 1965 with a "newly added Section 44B" requiring ride-hailing **vehicles** to hold route permits that are **district-specific and platform-specific**, renewed **annually**, applied for at each RTA. Industry proposed a single province-wide permit. **No source gives the amending instrument, effective date, fee, or evidence of enforcement.** No Sep/Oct 2026 update found. Treat as *enacted-but-status-unclear*.
5. **Police character certificate (May 2026).** Punjab government "signed a formal agreement" with ride-hailing companies: all drivers (motorcycle, rickshaw, car) must hold a Punjab Police character certificate before offering rides; platforms must collect it; a province-wide driver database is planned. Rs 500 at any Police Khidmat Markaz, ~3 working days, original CNIC + passport copy + affidavit. Sources: https://www.techjuice.pk/ride-hailing-drivers-need-police-certificate-in-punjab/; https://pakobserver.net/police-character-certificate-declared-mandatory-for-online-drivers/ (6-May-2026); https://pkm.punjab.gov.pk/public/home/services?id=a.
6. **QR panic button / GPS / dashcam (Lahore, Jun–Aug 2026).** Lahore Traffic Police (CTO) made PSCA's "CM Punjab QR Panic Button" mandatory for rickshaws, taxis and ride-hailing cars; enforcement from 1 Jul, deadline extended to 10 Jul 2026; GPS trackers and dashcams announced as phased requirements (no confirmed deadline). PSCA reports 35,900 devices province-wide (Dawn 7-Aug-2026) / 66,000+ in Lahore (LHR Times 31-Aug-2026); inDrive and Yango fleets in the ongoing phase. CTO on 31-Aug-2026 said action will be taken against online cab services not adopting safety features; "the compliance standard has not been published". Sources: https://propakistani.pk/2026/06/13/punjab-introduces-qr-panic-buttons-and-gps-tracking-to-enhance-ride-hailing-safety/; https://www.pakwheels.com/blog/lahore-makes-qr-panic-code-mandatory-for-ride-hailing-cars-and-rickshaws/; https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/06/deadline-extended-for-installing-qr-panic-buttons-in-online-taxis/; https://www.dawn.com/news/2021085; https://www.lhrtimes.com/2026/08/31/cto-lahore-warns-ride-hailing-firms-as-panic-button-rollout-crosses-66000-vehicles/; https://qrpanicbutton.psca.gop.pk.
7. **Conventional permit mechanics.** Motor-cab taxi permit: Rs 850 first district + Rs 100 per extra district + Rs 650 application fee, 1-year validity (a 25-Mar-2024 notification allows 3-year motor-cab permits — https://transport.punjab.gov.pk/system/files/NOTIFICATION.pdf, UNVERIFIED contents); rickshaw/qingqi Rs 600/yr + Rs 650 application. Documents: VICS fitness certificate (commercial fitness renewed 6-monthly), **third-party insurance**, token tax proof, registration book, CNIC, affidavit; apply at DRTA or https://pdtg-transport.punjab.gov.pk/route-permit. (pakera.pk guides 1–2 Oct 2026, secondary; https://transport.punjab.gov.pk/route_permits.)
8. **Insurance linkage.** From 1 Apr 2026 Punjab's route-permit system is integrated with CDC's Motor Insurance Repository; valid third-party insurance is auto-checked before any public-transport permit is issued/renewed (https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/04/01/punjab-enforces-mandatory-motor-insurance-through-digital-permit-system-integration/; https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/27/valid-3rd-party-insurance-is-now-mandatory-for-public-transport-vehicles-in-punjab/).
9. **Bikes / rickshaws.** No source states whether 44B covers motorcycles. The police-certificate agreement explicitly covers motorcycles and rickshaws. Helmets mandatory for rider and pillion in Punjab; from Jul 2026 Lahore impounds bikes for one day for helmet violations (Daily Pakistan 4-Jul-2026). Bykea continues to market bike rides in Lahore — no regulator statement on bike-taxi legality found.
10. **Islamabad (ICT) comparison.** From 10 Feb 2026 the DC Islamabad requires every ride-hailing/bike-hailing driver to register in the "CITY Islamabad" app (CNIC + vehicle docs), display a QR code, and log each passenger's name/CNIC before a trip; registrations later moved to an Islamabad Police Taxi Verification App (Mar 2026). (https://propakistani.pk/2026/02/11/islamabad-makes-online-registration-mandatory-for-ride-hailing-services/; Daily Pakistan 16-Mar-2026.)
11. **Sindh comparison.** Only a 2024-era announcement of a "unified system" for Karachi online taxis and a 2019 draft bill; no 2025/26 Sindh notification found. Sindh has made **third-party insurance mandatory for all vehicles** via Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2026 (SECP press release 7-Mar-2026 https://www.secp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Press-Release-March-7-SECP-Engagement-Leads-to-Mandatory-Motor-Third-Party-Insurance-in-Sindh-002.pdf).

**What it means for the product:** A **PTA-Punjab TNC operating licence is almost certainly required before launch** (Yango precedent; s.72-H). *[Closure note: the licence requirement stays an external legal gate; the "precedent" is a secondary report needing official reconciliation (CL-21).]* Budget for a large licence fee (reported Rs 2 m / 3 yrs, UNVERIFIED), an incorporated company with MoA/AoA, a documented passenger safety protocol and complaints mechanism. Driver onboarding must handle: CNIC, police character certificate (Rs 500), driving licence, vehicle registration, VICS fitness, token tax, third-party insurance, and (if 44B is enforced) a per-district route permit. Plan for mandatory PSCA QR panic code display and the in-app 15 call path. Model vehicles with multiple district permits with expiry; support export of driver lists to Transport Department/PSCA.

**Confidence:** Medium that a TNC licence is required and obtainable; **Low** on fees, 44B status/enforcement, and licence conditions.

(6) Status of the police-certificate "formal agreement".

## B. Personal data protection

**Status:** The **Personal Data Protection Bill (2023 draft; a 2025 draft also circulated) has NOT been enacted** as of the latest sources (Chambers guide 10-Mar-2026; Express Tribune op-ed 24-Aug-2026 still treats it as pending). No NCPDP regulator exists. Draft obligations (for design alignment only): explicit consent for sensitive/critical data; cross-border transfer only with adequacy/consent/NCPDP mechanism; "critical" data localised in Pakistan; 72-hour breach notice; children's data restrictions; limits on harmful automated profiling. Sources: https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan; https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=about&c=PK; https://tribune.com.pk/story/2625470/pakistan-can-no-longer-wait-for-personal-data-protection-law; https://moitt.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Final%20Draft%20Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Bill%20May%202023.pdf.

**PECA 2016 as amended 29-Jan-2025:** creates the Social Media Protection and Regulatory Authority (SMPRA) (not yet operational per TechJuice, Oct 2026), the NCCIA (replaces FIA Cyber Crime Wing), new s.26A "false information" offence, and an enlistment power over "social media platforms" defined broadly. Existing PTA registration duty: Rule 7, Removal and Blocking of Unlawful Online Content Rules 2021 — written for social media, but the broad definition is a risk for any app with chat/ratings. Sources: https://rsilpak.org/2025/2025-amendments-to-the-prevention-of-electronic-crimes-act-2016-an-introduction/; https://nchr.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/NCHR-Report-on-PECA-and-the-2025-Amendments-Act.pdf; https://www.techjuice.pk/social-media-companies-ignore-pta-registration-compliance-five-years/.

**Call/audio recording:** Supreme Court (reported ProPakistani 10-Mar-2026) held covert recording of private conversation without consent is criminal; secondary sources cite PECA ss.19–23 inconsistently. In-ride audio recording must be consent-based with in-app notice to both parties.

**What it means:** No binding privacy statute today, but build to the draft (consent screens, data minimisation, ability to host CNIC/biometric/location data in Pakistan, 72-h breach playbook, no child tracking). Expect NCCIA data requests under PECA s.32/33 — have a lawful-request process.
**Confidence:** High on non-enactment; Medium on PECA platform scope.

## C. Telecom / SMS

PTA's Protection from Spam, Unsolicited, Fraudulent and Obnoxious Communication Regulations 2009 plus operator rules; 2019 consultation proposed stricter opt-in — adoption UNVERIFIED. Practice (aggregator sources, Jan 2026): alphanumeric sender ID ("mask") must be approved by PTA, then registered with each MNO (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone); needs SECP-registered company, NTN, letterhead/stamp, CNIC of signatory; ~10–15 working days; mask must match company name; unused masks blocked after ~3 months. OTP/transactional traffic is exempt from the promotional 9 am–9 pm window; promotional needs opt-in and must be scrubbed against the DNCR (3627). Telenor/Ufone route A2P via GMS and may deliver from short codes. WhatsApp Business API is available (Meta policy governs templates/OTP). Sources: https://www.telerivet.com/blog/pakistan-sms-compliance-pta (31-Jan-2026); https://sendpk.com/pta-compliance.php; https://brandedsmspakistan.com/brand-name-registration/; https://www.pta.gov.pk/en/media-center/single-media/pta-issues-regulations-for-unsolicited-and-obnoxious-communications.

**What it means:** Register the Wayvro mask early (needs incorporation + NTN — ~2–4 weeks critical path); use a PTA-compliant aggregator; keep OTP and promo on separate categories; DNCR scrubbing; WhatsApp as a secondary OTP channel.
**Confidence:** Medium (vendor sources).

## D. Identity / KYC

**NADRA:** Verisys and biometric verification are offered to "government and regulated private-sector entities" under NADRA Ordinance s.7, NADRA (Data Sharing) Regulations 2025 and NADRA (Identity Verification) Regulations 2026, via the **Nishan Pakistan** portal (launched 10-Feb-2026, https://nishan.nadra.gov.pk): digital onboarding review of legal status, regulatory permissions and use case; APIs for fingerprint/face, Verisys, Proof-of-Life, SSO. Listed eligible classes are banks, MFBs, EMIs, NBFIs, telcos and "other regulators' licensees" — **a PTA-Punjab TNC licence may be the regulatory hook, but ride-hailing eligibility is UNVERIFIED.** Fees unpublished; stale 2021 datapoint: Rs 35/transaction + Rs 5,000 setup. Sources: https://propakistani.pk/2026/02/10/nadra-introduces-nishan-pakistan-to-strengthen-secure-identity-verification/; https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/nadra-launches-unified-platform-to-standardize-id-verification-services; https://psba.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/PSBA-Notices-036..pdf. Third-party CNIC "lookup" resellers are unlicensed — avoid.

**Driving licence:** Punjab DLIMS public verification at https://dlims.punjab.gov.pk; no public API. **Vehicle:** MTMIS Punjab public lookup at https://mtmis.excise-punjab.gov.pk; no public API; data access by agency MoU. **Police certificate:** see A.5. **Biometrics/CNIC copies:** no specific statute; draft PDP Bill would classify as sensitive data.

**What it means:** Manual/OCR CNIC capture + DLIMS/MTMIS screen checks at launch; apply to Nishan Pakistan in parallel citing the TNC licence; never advertise "NADRA-verified" without an actual agreement.
**Confidence:** Medium.

## E. Company / tax

- **SECP:** Private Ltd or SMC (Pvt) Ltd via eZfile; Seventh Schedule fees revised effective 20-Apr-2026 (page 403 on fetch). Secondary 2026 guides: online incorporation ~Rs 6,655 at ≤Rs 100k capital, name reservation Rs 1,000 (SECP FAQ still shows Rs 200 — conflict), 3–10 working days; SMC needs a nominee director. UNVERIFIED exact fees.
- **NTN/STRN:** FBR IRIS NTN post-incorporation; PRA registration for Punjab services.
- **PRA sales tax:** Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012, Second Schedule — "ride-hailing services" (serial 69, includes "cab aggregators" per 12-Sep-2023 notification) taxed at **5% without input adjustment** (KPMG Provincial Tax Brief Jul 2025 https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/pk/pdf/2025/07/A-Brief-of-Provincial-Tax-Laws-2025.pdf; EY https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/2024-1353-pakistan-adopts-changes-to-provincial-sales-tax-laws). Whether the 5% applies to gross fare or platform commission is **UNVERIFIED** — key question.
- **FBR:** Finance Act 2025 inserted s.6A + s.153(2A): 1% (digital payment, withheld by payment intermediary) / 2% (COD, withheld by courier) on "digitally ordered goods or services" via online marketplaces; Finance Act 2026 makes it adjustable above Rs 200 m turnover (KPMG Brief Jul 2026; FBR Circular 01/2025-26). Application to ride fares/driver payouts UNVERIFIED. "Online marketplace" definition (s.2(38B)) may capture Wayvro with reporting duties (s.165C).
- **Gig workers:** No platform-worker law; drivers are independent contractors under Contract Act 1872; an ICT platform-workers bill is pending (https://www.mondaq.com/employee-benefits-compensation/1730122/).
- **Incentives:** PSEB registration optional; export incentives not relevant to domestic ride revenue.

**What it means:** Incorporate Pvt Ltd first (gates NTN → PRA → SMS mask → TNC licence). *[Closure note: founder decision FD-10 — incorporation starts now, but the form is not hard-coded: a sole founder evaluates an SECP Single Member Company; an ordinary private limited company needs an appropriate multi-member structure; no nominee is invented; final choice with counsel/accountant.]* Model 5% PRA on rides and possible 1% s.6A withholding by payment gateway; drivers as contractors with a clear service agreement.
**Confidence:** Medium (rates), Low (PRA base; s.6A scope).

## F. Insurance

Third-party motor insurance is legally required but ~97% of vehicles uninsured (SECP chairman). Enforcement is now mechanical for **public-transport/permit vehicles in Punjab** (MIR integration, Apr 2026) and for **all vehicles in Sindh** (2026). Ride-hailing products: **inDrive** – "EFU Loyalty In-Ride Insurance" (https://www.efulife.com/assets/documents/inDrive.pdf); **Yango** – free per-trip insurance via insurtech **Waada** since Sep 2024 (https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2024/09/26/yango-launches-free-ride-insurance-in-partnership-with-waada/); **Bykea** – advertises customer/partner/parcel cover (https://bykea.com/insurance/, 403).

**What it means:** Require third-party cover per vehicle at onboarding; negotiate a group per-trip personal-accident policy (EFU/Waada model) before launch — competitors already advertise it.
**Confidence:** High on legal requirement/enforcement; Medium on products.

## G. Emergency services

Police 15 and Rescue 1122 have **no public API/developer programme**. PSCA runs the Emergency 15 centre, Public Safety App, Women Safety App 2.0, the QR panic button and a Virtual Women Police Station (https://psca.gop.pk). Women's helpline 1043 is the Punjab Commission on the Status of Women. The only platform integration found is **Yango–PSCA**: MoU May 2025, then Jan 2026 in-app "Emergency 15" call routed to PSCA with priority flag and automatic push of rider location, pickup/drop-off, driver identity and vehicle registration (https://www.thenews.pk/print/1391214-yango-links-emergency-15-to-ride-hailing-app).

**What it means:** Ship a dialer-based "Call 15" + live trip share at launch; pursue a bespoke PSCA MoU for a data-push integration. Do not claim integration until signed and tested.
**Confidence:** High (no API), Medium (MoU route).
