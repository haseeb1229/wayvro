# 22 — Launch Blockers and External Dependencies

These are **not solvable by writing more code**. They must remain blocked until real evidence exists.

**Phase 0 update (2026-10-09):** the current, sourced blocker list with owners, gates and status — including the Punjab TNC operating licence, Section 44B district permits, police character certificates, the PSCA QR panic-button mandate, the unread 19-Feb-2026 TNC safety protocols, SBP/PSP decisions, store policy deadlines and the biometric DPIA — is `docs/phase0/PHASE0_LAUNCH_BLOCKERS.md`. This file keeps the category structure.

## Legal/regulatory
- Punjab TNC operating license / company authorization applicable to the launch model.
- Current route-permit scope for each active ride-hailing vehicle/district/platform.
- Vehicle registration/authorized use, token tax, fitness and required insurance.
- Driver licence/category/endorsement, training/background/medical requirements under current rules.
- Legal review for women-only/pooling/minors/recording/biometrics/location retention and driver contracting.
- Tax/consumer/corporate agreements and regulator-data-request process.

## Vendors/commercial
- Production SMS/OTP and fallback provider with Pakistan delivery metrics and abuse controls.
- Maps/geocoding/directions/navigation license, pricing and Lahore field accuracy.
- PSP/merchant contracts for any digital payment; Raast through eligible regulated participant; payout rails.
- KYC/liveness/background providers if automation is used, with DPA, accuracy, appeals and exit path.
- Insurance/takaful agreement before advertising coverage.
- Masked-call/WhatsApp transactional providers if those channels are enabled.

## Human operations
- Real safety escalation staff and emergency procedures.
- Driver vetting/training/field support capacity.
- Customer support, finance reconciliation and incident ownership.
- Qualified security/privacy/transport/legal launch reviews.
- Driver supply density and pilot economics.

## Store/production
- Apple/Google developer accounts and store approval for the **single multi-role Wayvro app**, including accurate data-safety/privacy declarations, account deletion, driver-only background-location justification and just-in-time permission behavior.
- Production DNS/domain/email, secrets, monitoring/on-call, backups/restore and paid capacity where free tiers are not reliable enough.

A feature may be fully coded and still remain `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`. The app must hide/disable it until its blocker evidence is approved.

## Company/governance blockers
- Verified legal entity/authorized signatories, IP chain/cap table/company account custody appropriate to financing/contracting stage.
- Accountant/legal confirmation of actual tax/filing/invoicing/withholding obligations for the chosen entity and service model.
- Personnel access/offboarding, privileged workstation controls and maker-checker capacity before multiple staff handle KYC/money/safety.
- Independent security assessment/penetration remediation appropriate to the exposure before real-user public launch.
